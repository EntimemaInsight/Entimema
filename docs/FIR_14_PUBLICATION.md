## Methodology and reading architecture

The opening distinguishes a sophisticated, trusted model from an analyst-dependent operating sequence. The executive thesis, model-versus-workflow comparison, assessment matrix, four-layer migration visual, worked finding and final synthesis provide the executive reading path. Sections provide a framework path and the full practitioner detail without three redundant summaries.

A model represents financial relationships; a workflow governs recurring execution, prerequisites, state, controls, evidence, ownership and release. Excel remains appropriate for exploration, scenarios, prototypes, low-frequency work, small controlled populations and cases where migration costs exceed control benefits.

Seven distinct risk mechanisms: formula, link, data, version, process, knowledge and governance. Controls address the actual failure mechanism rather than treating every workbook as unsafe. Analyst expertise remains valuable; undocumented execution dependency is the risk.

Assessment considers supported decisions, materiality, recurrence, source complexity and stability, logic stability, manual steps, formula complexity, mappings, exceptions, review, knowledge dependency, version conflict, lineage, recurring outputs, migration effort and control benefit. Recommendations are retain Excel, strengthen Excel controls, hybrid workflow or governed workflow. Technical feasibility, economics, control improvement and adoption are separate tests.

Two-layer architecture: a flexible analytical layer above governed execution. Sources → extraction → harmonisation → canonical mapping → deterministic calculations and reconciliation → validated output → optional Excel consumption. Proposed analytical changes return through definition, testing, review, approval and versioning.

Migration: Observe → Stabilise → Externalise Intake → Externalise Logic → Add Controls → Add Workflow State → Validated Output → Excel Export. Eight steps are grouped into four responsive layers. Monitoring continues throughout. Exit tests, safe reruns, invalidation, parallel comparisons, authoritative cutover and recovery are explicit; a hybrid boundary can remain the endpoint.

Nineteen failure/control rows cover premature replacement, broken sequences, opaque code, overmigration, lost reviewability, lost flexibility, absent lineage, uncontrolled mappings, meaningless state, hidden exceptions, export provenance, local edits, conflicting engines, big-bang migration, time-only metrics, vendor dependency, static-model confusion and AI-led justification.

## Fictional worked example

Seven current-cycle inputs plus a retained prior-period report support monthly manufacturing profitability. The prior report is not counted again as a current-period cost source.

- Reported Product Family A contribution: EUR **1,840,000**.
- Omitted product material cost: EUR **110,000**, retained in the company unallocated pool.
- Stale energy-allocation driver: EUR **90,000**, overallocated to other products.
- Incorrect material mapping: EUR **70,000**, assigned to Product Family B.
- Total overstatement: **110,000 + 90,000 + 70,000 = EUR 270,000**.
- Corrected Product Family A contribution: **1,840,000 − 270,000 = EUR 1,570,000**.
- Other families: EUR **2,050,000 → 2,210,000**.
- Unallocated production costs: EUR **−110,000 → 0**.
- Company contribution: EUR **3,780,000 → 3,780,000**; unchanged other expenses preserve P&L agreement.

The omission affects the product schedule, not company ledger completeness. The energy pool is current actual cost allocated with an outdated driver. The hard-coded correction is included in that same EUR 90,000 error, not an extra component; the schema change is not an additional quantified loss. Contribution is explicitly defined under the example's management allocation policy.

The migrated process registers expected sources, detects schema change, retains raw kg observations and the divide-by-1,000 tonne transformation, governs mapping and allocation versions, reconciles populations and totals, routes new-material review and releases an evidence-linked finding and Excel workbook. Classification tests remain separate from company-total reconciliation.

## Export and product boundary

Exports retain validated data, mappings, comparatives, metrics, transformations, exceptions, findings, evidence references, decision-readiness status, stable identifiers, versions and timestamps. Governed baseline, assumptions, scenarios and editable analysis remain distinct. Local edits are not automatically governed; re-entry is a proposed change against a retained baseline.

Entimema Financial Intelligence is positioned as governing repeatable financial logic while preserving Excel's analytical role. Model intelligence proposes structural and semantic interpretations, mappings and clarification. Deterministic code owns arithmetic, transformations, approved allocation, reconciliation and fixed controls. Human judgement owns material policy, exceptions, scope and decisions.

The architecture is explicitly subject to scoping and validation, not a claim that every capability, macro, add-in, export or integration is deployed. No universal compatibility, one-click migration, time-saving rate, accuracy rate or production SLA is asserted.

Recurring value comes from executing new cycles with current evidence and retained reviewable, reversible, scope-controlled context. It is not a pricing promotion.
