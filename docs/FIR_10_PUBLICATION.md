## Methodology and editorial verification

The opening day-five promise exposes day-four document completion without numerical readiness. Four existing framework layers connect scope/source control, financial structuring, control/analysis and decision/closure. A 21-stage contract supplies input, owner, control, output, blocking failure and evidence. Separate accounting and reporting state paths prohibit a closed ledger or conversational completion message from standing in for decision readiness.

Completeness uses applicable expected sources and current authoritative valid versions, never only received files. Missing sources remain exceptions, not zero. Scope-controlled mappings preserve original values and evidence. Deterministic controls cover source totals, trial balance, Balance Sheet, P&L, subledgers, intercompany, periods, planning comparability, mapping and reports. Adjustments remain a separate reversible layer; ledger postings cannot double-count reporting overlays. Exceptions carry operating ownership and downstream blocking effects.

Commentary follows validated findings and is invalidated by dependent numerical changes. Approval binds a defined object and version; immutable publication and explicit supersession retain history. The calendar captures dependencies and escalation. Nine purpose-specific readiness states, a speed/quality/stability matrix and twenty concise failure/control rows support executive scanning and practitioner execution without separate repeated summaries.

### Original fictional example

- Three entities; twelve logical sources: three trial balances and nine group schedules, each covering the entities.
- Initial receipt: 12/12. Valid authoritative coverage: 10/12 because one trial balance and the budget are superseded. Replacement establishes 12/12 on day two; reconciliation remains a separate gate.
- Eight exceptions: early trial balance, new account, inventory difference EUR 180,000, intercompany difference EUR 75,000, unsupported accrual EUR 240,000, obsolete budget, stale margin commentary, and EUR 30,000 payroll classification uncertainty.
- Inventory receipt correction: debit inventory / credit trade payables EUR 180,000, no immediate P&L effect. A separate evidenced EUR 90,000 write-down changes cost of sales and inventory.
- Revenue EUR 12.00m; gross profit EUR 3.60m minus EUR 0.09m = EUR 3.51m. Margin 30.00% → 29.25%, down 0.75 percentage points.
- Operating profit EUR 1.20m minus EUR 0.09m write-down minus EUR 0.24m administrative accrual = EUR 0.87m. Each expense is recognised once after accounting approval and removal of the provisional overlay.
- Intercompany correction debits the unmatched payable and credits recorded cash EUR 75,000. It is not another expense.
- Payroll uncertainty is material for functional accountability but immaterial to the selected group earnings decision. Qualified group use is approved; affected cost-centre comparison remains withheld.
- Fictional milestone comparison: completeness day 4 → 2; first reconciled model 6 → 3; commentary 7 → 4; approved publication 8 → 5; three post-publication corrections → zero material corrections. These are explicitly not product performance claims.

Recurring value comes from renewed monthly controls, evidence and approvals plus governed reusable mappings, owners and rules. Prior context remains reviewable, reversible and subordinate to current evidence. The Financial Intelligence bridge separates model interpretation, deterministic calculation/state rules and human judgement.
