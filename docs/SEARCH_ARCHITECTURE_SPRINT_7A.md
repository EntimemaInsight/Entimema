# Sprint 7A — Search Architecture and Keyword Strategy

Status: strategy foundation; no public content published in Sprint 7A

Site language: English only

Markets: Bulgaria and international English
Evidence boundary: this architecture is based on business fit, observable search-result categories, and current site capabilities. It does not claim keyword volumes or ranking difficulty. Validate demand with first-party Search Console data and a reputable keyword dataset before creating new pages.

## 1. Target markets

### Market A — Bulgaria

Decision-makers in Bulgaria may search in Bulgarian or English. English terminology is especially plausible for CFO, FP&A, ERP/SAP, analytics, credit risk, and AI concepts, but this must be verified rather than assumed. The current English site should capture relevant English-language commercial demand while Bulgarian demand is tested through external distribution and measurement.

### Market B — International English

Target organisations with complex finance, risk, data, and decision problems. International acquisition should favour narrow, expert-led intent over broad finance traffic. Geographic expansion should follow demonstrated authority and lead quality, not a generic global keyword footprint.

### Cluster boundaries to prevent cannibalisation

- CFO Advisory owns finance-function design and senior finance capability intent.
- Planning & Forecasting owns budgets, rolling forecasts, operational drivers, scenarios, and cash-planning systems.
- Management Reporting owns management information, KPI architecture, reporting rhythm, and decision-facing reporting.
- Cost & Margin Management owns cost models, allocation, manufacturing economics, margin, and profitability.
- Financial Data owns finance data architecture, ERP/SAP information foundations, reconciliation, and traceability—not software implementation.
- Credit Risk owns credit models, policy, portfolio behaviour, monitoring, and stress testing.
- Decision Intelligence owns cross-domain decision models, rules, engines, and governed execution; cluster-specific scenarios link to their finance or risk owner page.
- Finance AI Agents and Risk AI Agents own controlled agent workflows in their respective domains. They should not compete for generic “AI consulting.”
- AML & Compliance remains a commercial service adjacent to the six core search clusters; it owns AML control architecture, KYC/CDD, transaction monitoring, and investigations—not consumer compliance definitions.

## 6. Editorial keyword opportunities

The editorial layer should answer consequential problems and technical questions that demonstrate Entimema's operating knowledge. Priority territories are:

- forecasts failing because operational drivers, cash mechanics, or ownership are missing;
- working-capital and cash-conversion-cycle modelling;
- cost allocation, manufacturing cost architecture, margin leakage, and product/customer profitability;
- credit vintage behaviour, transition matrices, segmentation, deterioration, and stress testing;
- ERP/SAP data translated into consistent management information rather than another reporting layer;
- decision models, uncertainty, rules, and the passage from analytical output to governed action;
- controlled AI agents with trusted financial/risk context, explicit boundaries, auditability, and human escalation.

Editorial content should not become a definitions library. Each Resource must have a specific decision problem, a defensible expert point of view, and one primary commercial relationship.

### Positioning risks

- “Fractional CFO” results often bundle accounting, fundraising, and finance operations; careless targeting could misrepresent the offer.
- “Management accounting” can imply outsourced accounting or qualifications; use it within cost/decision contexts, not as an unqualified primary term.
- SAP and ERP searches are heavily occupied by software vendors and implementation partners; state the finance-information and decision layer clearly.
- AI terms are volatile and crowded by software/vendor content. Avoid generic futurism and anchor every claim in workflow, data, control, and accountability.
- Broad Bulgarian phrases such as “financial consultant” can span personal finance, grants, insurance, loans, and accounting. They are not safe primary targets without modifiers and evidence.

## 9. Content and architecture gaps

1. There is no indexable Resources hub, cluster page, or article route. The current “Resources” navigation resolves to a homepage anchor, so editorial search intent has no durable canonical destination.
2. No first-party organic measurement is visible in the repository. Demand, geography, query language, and conversion contribution cannot yet be evaluated.
3. Financial modelling has real commercial adjacency but no unambiguous page owner; promoting it across CFO and Planning pages could cause cannibalisation.
4. Working capital is strategically strong but lacks a clear commercial proposition and editorial authority path.
5. ERP/SAP content currently supports Financial Data, but no deep Resource demonstrates the difference between system implementation and finance-information architecture.
6. Credit Risk has strong technical authority territory, but the site currently lacks indexable content for vintage, transition, stress, and deterioration queries.
7. Decision Intelligence and AI-agent terminology are emerging. Their service pages establish positioning, but future content must explain concrete architectures and controls rather than chase volatile labels.
8. Bulgaria-specific demand and language behaviour remain unmeasured. A `/bg/` structure is therefore unjustified.

No low-risk live-page correction is required in Sprint 7A: the current indexable routes have English metadata, canonicals, sitemap inclusion, and clear service separation. Speculative metadata expansion would create more risk than value before query evidence exists.

## 12. Bulgarian-search recommendation

Keep the website English-only. Do not add `/bg/`, Bulgarian metadata, mixed-language page copy, hidden text, or doorway pages during Series 7A.

Use a staged evidence model:

1. Verify the site in Google Search Console and segment impressions/clicks by country, query, page, and device.
2. Observe whether Bulgarian buyers use English specialist language (for example CFO, FP&A, SAP, credit risk) and which current pages receive those impressions.
3. Distribute selected expert ideas in Bulgarian through founder/team LinkedIn posts or relevant professional channels, linking only when the English destination genuinely serves the reader.
4. Strengthen legitimate local signals: consistent organisation/contact information, credible Bulgarian professional profiles, earned local mentions, and relevant partnerships—not keyword manipulation.
5. Test Bulgarian topic demand and lead quality externally. Consider public Bulgarian editorial content only after sustained evidence shows a distinct audience that English content cannot serve.
6. If a language expansion is eventually justified, design it as a complete, user-visible, maintainable language experience with dedicated URLs, translated navigation, canonicals, and hreflang. It must not begin as isolated SEO pages.

Bulgarian query families should remain research inputs, not text inserted into English pages. High-risk broad terms such as “финансов консултант” require strong commercial modifiers because their intent is mixed.

## 13. International-search recommendation

- Lead with English commercial pages that already have precise canonical ownership.
- Prioritise specialist, mid- and long-tail combinations around a problem, method, industry context, or provider need rather than head terms alone.
- Establish authority in Cost & Profitability, Credit Risk, Financial Data/ERP, and driver-based Planning before trying to rank broadly for “financial consulting.”
- Treat CFO/fractional CFO as a qualified segment: differentiate financial architecture and decision systems from bookkeeping, fundraising-only, and generic startup finance packages.
- Use SAP/ERP terms to reach finance and transformation leaders, while making clear that Entimema designs the management-information and decision layer rather than selling an implementation shop.
- Develop Decision Intelligence and AI-agent topics as emerging authority positions. Measure impressions and qualified engagement before expanding the number of commercial pages.
- Avoid country landing pages until there is delivery capacity, evidence of meaningful demand, and genuinely localised value.

The observed landscape is fragmented among large advisory firms, specialist boutiques, accounting/fractional-CFO providers, credit-risk specialists, ERP/software vendors, and educational publishers. Entimema should not imitate any single category. Its defensible intersection is finance × risk × data × decision systems, expressed through technical depth and implementation-aware decision architecture.

## 14. Internal-linking architecture

Future structure:

`Resource article ↔ related Resource(s) ↔ Resource cluster/hub ↔ one primary service ↔ contact`

Rules:

- Every Resource has one primary cluster, one primary service owner, and an optional secondary service only when the relationship is real.
- Article links use descriptive, contextual anchors at the point where the capability becomes relevant; avoid repetitive site-wide SEO link blocks.
- Service pages link to a small set of directly relevant Resources as evidence of method and expertise.
- Resources link laterally only when the next article advances the reader's problem, not merely because it shares a keyword.
- Cluster hubs should explain the problem territory and curate pathways; they should not duplicate service-page commercial copy.
- Contact links should follow a meaningful diagnostic or commercial transition, carrying the relevant topic where the existing form supports it.
- Breadcrumbs and future structured data should reflect the visible hierarchy once a Resources architecture exists.
- One primary search intent has one canonical destination. If a query spans clusters, modifiers decide ownership.

Recommended future route shape, subject to Sprint 7B architecture review:

- `/resources` — indexable editorial hub;
- `/resources/{cluster}` — optional only if each cluster can support a useful curated page;
- `/resources/{article-slug}` — canonical article route.

Do not create empty cluster pages or thin archives solely to increase URL count.

## 15. Measurement requirements

### Current readiness

- Indexable routes have explicit canonical URLs.
- `robots.txt` allows crawling and points to the canonical sitemap.
- The sitemap lists the current English public routes.
- Page metadata is present and differentiated at service level.
- No analytics or Search Console integration is represented in the repository; none was fabricated in Sprint 7A.

### Required in a later measurement sprint

1. Verify all canonical host variants in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.
2. Record a pre-content baseline by query, landing page, country, device, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position.
3. Define consent-aware analytics requirements appropriate to the site's legal and operating context before adding any script.
4. Track meaningful stages without treating every click as a lead:
   `organic impression → organic click → Resource/service visit → contact intent → valid form submission → qualified lead`.
5. Define contact intent events (for example, contact CTA activation and form start) and a successful server-confirmed submission event; exclude validation errors and bots.
6. Preserve UTM/referrer and landing-page context through the inquiry flow where privacy requirements permit.
7. Connect qualified-lead outcomes outside the website so content is evaluated on relevance and commercial contribution, not traffic alone.
8. Create a query-to-canonical-page review that identifies cannibalisation, unexpected language/country patterns, and irrelevant search traffic.
9. Review emerging AI/Decision Intelligence terminology separately because impressions may reflect category curiosity rather than buyer intent.
10. Set review periods suitable for low-volume expert B2B search; avoid conclusions from short windows or statistically weak samples.
